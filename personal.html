Some history about myself: I grew up in the suburbs of Beirut (Lebanon), in a pretty poor family and neighbourhood. My parents are uneducated (both didn’t complete middle school). My father was a carpenter who died more than 10 years ago, and my mother is a homemaker. I have an older and younger brother in a similar age bracket. My family was pretty dysfunctional, in the sense that daily physical and verbal abuse was the norm. Many people assume that my family’s religious background is muslim, which is fair given that most of the Arab world is. But mine is from the Maronite Christianity faith. I don’t think it’s much better in terms of personal liberties. It was definitely tough growing up in such an environment, and my family didn’t really support my ambitions as a woman, even when I excelled at school. They thought for more or less okayish reasons (didn’t seem to me so at the time), I would be better off quitting education, and marry a more wealthy man and be a homemaker.
I guess that might sound like drama for some humans, so let’s switch to something more fun?! Or is it?!
Regarding my value system: I’m an atheist, I favor center-left/social democratic ideology, I’m a feminist, a minimalist, and I care about the environment. I also think people should be nice to one another or at least try to.
